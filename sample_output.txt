# Trail Briefing: Ricketts Glen Falls Trail
*Benton, PA · 7.2 mi · 1,200 ft gain · Fall · Moderate*

## Overview
A moderate day out: 7.2 miles and 1,200 ft of gain asks for steady pacing and a real lunch stop. Expect about 4.2 hours of moving time, plus stops.

## Pacing plan
Start no later than 8 AM — with 4.2 hours of moving time plus breaks, you want a buffer before dark.
Climb steadily and save something for the descent — most injuries happen on tired legs going down.

## Pack list
- Navigation: map + compass/GPS (phone dies, paper doesn't)
- 2+ liters of water and calorie-dense food
- Insulating mid-layer — temps swing 20°F+ through the day
- Headlamp with fresh batteries (shorter days sneak up on you)
- Bright colors — it's hunting season in many areas

## Watch-outs
Elevation: 1,200 ft of gain means weather can differ sharply from the trailhead — carry a layer even if it starts warm.
Daylight is the constraint: sunset comes early and temperatures drop fast once the sun dips. Leaf-covered rocks are slippery.

## For you
Pace by effort on climbs, not speed; eat before you're hungry and drink before you're thirsty.

## Leave No Trace
Pack it in, pack it out — and stay on trail through muddy sections.
